Photograph by Aydın Tiryaki (aydintiryaki.org), used on this site with his
permission, given to Emre Çevik on 2026-09-10.

It was suggested as a picture of the U3 amphitheatre and may well be one --
three brick volumes with copper roofs is what a "triple amphitheatre"
should look like. But the photographer could not place it with certainty
either, and no source ties the file to a building. So the page captions it
as the campus, which it certainly is, and not as the hall, which nobody has
confirmed. If it is later identified, only the caption string needs to
change.

Replaced: Atılım Güneş Baydın's 2003 public-domain view from the MM
building, which was a stand-in while no permission-cleared photograph
existed.
