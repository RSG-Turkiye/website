The 2nd RSG-Türkiye Student Symposium was held on 27 September 2013 at the METU Cultural and Convention Center in Ankara, as a satellite meeting to HIBIT'13.

## Keynote Speakers

- **Francesca Ciccarelli** — European Institute of Oncology and the European School of Molecular Medicine, Milan, on the systems-level properties of cancer genes and what they tell us about cancer drivers
- **Lars Juhl Jensen** — Novo Nordisk Foundation Center for Protein Research, Copenhagen, on networks of proteins and diseases, and the STRING database

## Programme

Four student presentations opened the afternoon — Sibel Deniz on binding motifs in effector proteins of the Go alpha subunit, H. Melike Dönertaş on multiplicity in the Enzyme Commission system, Cemal Ulaş on sequence redundancy in tandem mass spectra, and Aybuge Altay on the quality assessment of tandem mass spectra.

A session on the state of the field followed: Zeynep Özkeserli and Dinçer Göksülük on bioinformatics in Türkiye from an academic point of view, Gökmen Zararsız on Turkish bioinformatics researchers abroad, and A. Raşit Öztürk on the industrial view. The day closed with an industrial networking session.

## About

ISCB-SC RSG-Türkiye is a student-led, non-profit organization affiliated with the International Society for Computational Biology Student Council (ISCB-SC). Our annual symposiums have been bringing together undergraduate and graduate students since 2012.
