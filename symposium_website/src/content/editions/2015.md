The 3rd RSG-Türkiye Student Symposium was held on 17 October 2015 in Muğla, as a satellite meeting to HIBIT 2015, which was hosted by Muğla Sıtkı Koçman University. The day included a science slam — a short, informal session where attendees introduced themselves and their work — which became a fixture of later symposiums.

ISCB SC RSG-Türkiye is a student-led, non-profit organization affiliated with the International Society for Computational Biology Student Council (ISCB SC). Our annual symposiums have been bringing together undergraduate and graduate students since 2012.
