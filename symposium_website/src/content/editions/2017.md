The 4th RSG-Türkiye Student Symposium was held on 30 June 2017 at METU Northern Cyprus Campus in Güzelyurt, as a satellite session to HiBiT 2017. The programme opened with a keynote by Dr. Uğur Sezerman of Acıbadem University, continued with a student session, and closed with a talk by Dr. Emre Güney of Universitat Pompeu Fabra. Alongside it ran a poster contest, judged by HiBiT participants, with three prizes.

ISCB-SC RSG-Türkiye is a student-led, non-profit organization affiliated with the International Society for Computational Biology Student Council (ISCB-SC). Our annual symposiums have been bringing together undergraduate and graduate students since 2012.
