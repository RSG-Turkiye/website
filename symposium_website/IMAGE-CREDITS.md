## `public/images/metu-campus.jpg`

Part of the METU campus seen from the MM building (Mühendislik Merkez
Binası), photographed 7 May 2003 by **Atılım Güneş Baydın**, who released it
into the public domain.

<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MiddleEastTechnicalUniversityCampus800x470.jpg>

The caption calls it the campus rather than the U3 amphitheatre, because
that is what can be verified about it. `venue.photoCaption` in
`src/i18n/ui.ts` is the string to change if a photograph of the hall itself
replaces this one.

A different photograph stood here for part of 2026-09-10 and was taken down
the same day, once it turned out the person who had offered it was not its
photographer. Nobody has been able to say who is, so there is no one to ask
for permission — which is the whole reason it went. Worth recording only so
that the same file is not proposed again as already cleared.
