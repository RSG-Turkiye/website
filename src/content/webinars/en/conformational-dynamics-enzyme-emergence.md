**Presenter**

**Prof. Dr. Lynn Kamerlin**

Prof. Dr. Lynn Kamerlin received her MSci in Natural Sciences from the University of Birmingham, UK, in 2002, where she went on to a PhD in Theoretical Organic Chemistry under the supervision of Dr. John Wilkie (awarded 2005). She subsequently worked in the laboratory of Stefan Boresch at the University of Vienna (2005–2007), in the laboratory of Arieh Warshel at the University of Southern California (2007–2009), and as a Researcher with Fahmi Himo (2010). She is currently Professor and Georgia Research Alliance – Vasser Wooley Chair of Molecular Design at Georgia Tech, a Fellow of the Royal Society of Chemistry, and a Senior Editor of Protein Science. She is also a Wallenberg Scholar, held an ERC Starting Independent Researcher Grant (2012–2017), and served as Chair of the Young Academy of Europe (YAE) in 2014–2015.
