**Presenters**

In this episode we welcome the students of Koç University.

- **Simge Şenyüz** — drug-like molecule structures on protein–protein interaction interfaces
- **Cansu Demirel** — network commonalities and differences between cancer and neurodevelopmental disorders
- **Enes Sefa Ayar** — modelling tumour progression through networks, and personalised medicine
- **Melisa Ece Zeylan** — elucidating the important pathways in vascular cognitive impairment (VCI)
- **Sina Dadmand** — oncological drug response prediction with network embedding
