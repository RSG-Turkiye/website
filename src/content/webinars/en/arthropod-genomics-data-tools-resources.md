**Presenter**

**Gloria Isabel Giraldo-Calderón, PhD** — Scientific Liaison / Outreach Manager, VectorBase

**Abstract**

Arthropods are the largest and most diverse eukaryotic phylum, with more than a million species. This talk covers the progress of genomic sequencing for them: over 360 whole arthropod genomes and more than 1000 transcriptomes are currently available, including large-scale projects on multiple strains of Tetranychus urticae, Anopheles gambiae variants, hemipteroids and Daphnia.

It gives an overview of the bioinformatic resources specialising in arthropod data — VectorBase, AphidBase, Ensembl Metazoa, NCBI-GenBank, OrthoDB and others — and the practical considerations of accessing and using both published and unpublished genomic datasets in research.
