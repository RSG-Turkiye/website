You approve and schedule the posts the editors prepare. The publisher role includes the editor role.

## What you can do

- In the Awaiting approval tab you decide on posts with Approve and schedule or Send back.
- You schedule your own posts directly with Schedule.
- You cancel posts. In the Event mode tab you manage event windows with Open window and Close window.
- You see the Messages tab.

## What you cannot do, and who to ask

- An admin manages the connections (Instagram, LinkedIn, X). If one breaks, tell an admin.
- An admin grants the role. It lasts at most 12 months, then is renewed.
- You cannot delete a published post from the panel. Delete it on the platform itself.

## Common tasks

Approving a post:

1. Open the post in the Awaiting approval tab.
2. Check the text and the image.
3. Choose Approve and schedule.

Sending back:

1. Choose Send back.
2. Write what needs to change in the note.
3. Choose Send back with this note. The editor sees the note.

## Common problems

- A post more than 2 hours late does not go out on its own. Choose Publish anyway.
- A failed post is retried every minute for 2 hours. If it sticks, choose Retry.
- Once publishing has started, the post cannot be edited or cancelled. Use Retry.
- An Instagram story needs a Business account. If a connection warning appears, tell an admin.
