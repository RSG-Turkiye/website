You approve and schedule the posts the editors prepare. The publisher role comes with the editor role.

## What you can do

- In the **Awaiting approval** tab you decide on posts: **Approve and schedule** or **Send back**.
- You schedule your own posts directly with **Schedule**.
- You cancel posts.
- In the **Event mode** tab you manage event windows with **Open window** and **Close window**.
- You see the **Messages** tab.

## What you cannot do, and who to ask

- An admin manages the Instagram, LinkedIn and X connections. If one breaks, tell an admin.
- An admin grants the role. It lasts up to 12 months or is permanent. If it has an end date, an admin renews it before then.
- You cannot delete a published post from the panel. Delete it on the platform's own page.

## Common tasks

Approving a post:

1. Open the **Awaiting approval** tab and choose the post.
2. Check the text and the image.
3. Press **Approve and schedule**.

Sending back:

1. Press **Send back**.
2. Write what needs to change in the note.
3. Press **Send back with this note**. The editor sees the note.

## Common problems

- A post that is more than 2 hours past its planned time does not go out on its own. If you want it to go out, press **Publish anyway**.
- A failed post is retried every minute for 2 hours. If it still has not gone out, press **Retry**.
- Once a post has started to go out, it can no longer be edited or cancelled. If it is stuck, press **Retry**.
- An Instagram story needs a Business account. If a connection warning appears, tell an admin.
