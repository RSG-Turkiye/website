You see and manage the whole panel. You are the one who grants roles.

## What you can do

- Users and roles, Roles: find a person and give a role with Add. For the duration choose 1 month, several months, a date or Permanent.
- You may grant every role in use. Leader roles (admin, announcements, symposium, social media publisher, content approver) last up to 12 months, or Permanent. Editor roles last at most one month.
- Connections: you see the state of the Gmail and social media accounts and reconnect them when needed.
- Activity log and Statistics: you see what happened and the counts.
- On the Overview you watch the free plan's daily usage (Free plan, today (UTC)).

## What you cannot do, and who to ask

- You cannot remove your own admin role. Keep at least two admins.
- For technical problems, read the `docs/handover/` folder in the repository. Your AI assistant can read it too.
- Your assistant cannot approve, publish or grant roles. Only you can.

## Common tasks

Granting a role:

1. Open Users and roles, Roles.
2. Under the role, find the person and choose the Duration.
3. Choose Add.

Renewing a role:

1. Find the person on the Roles page.
2. Choose Renew and pick the new end date. Roles end by themselves on their end date, so renew before it.

Keeping the records:

1. Open the Activity log page.
2. Choose Export JSON and keep it with the year's records. The panel keeps only a year of history.

## Common problems

- If a warning shows on the Overview, look at the Connections page. Gmail or an account may have disconnected.
- When usage reaches 70% or 90%, the admins get an e-mail. You read today's numbers on the Overview.
- If saved content does not show on the site, read `docs/handover/troubleshooting.md`.
