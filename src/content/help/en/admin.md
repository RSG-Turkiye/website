You see and manage the whole panel. You are the one who grants roles.

## What you can do

- Open the **Roles** page under **Users and roles**, find the person and give the role with **Add**. For **Duration** choose 1 month, several months, a date or **Permanent**.
- You may grant all roles. Leader roles (admin, announcements, symposium, publisher, content approver) last up to 12 months or permanently. Editor roles last at most one month.
- On the **Connections** page you see the state of the Gmail and social media accounts and reconnect them when needed.
- On the **Activity log** and **Statistics** pages you see what happened.
- On the **Overview** page the **Free plan, today (UTC)** box shows the daily usage.

## What you cannot do, and who to ask

- You cannot remove your own admin role. Keep at least two admins.
- For technical problems, read the `docs/handover/` folder in the repository.
- Your assistant cannot approve, publish or grant roles.

## Common tasks

Granting a role:

1. Open the **Roles** page under **Users and roles**.
2. Under the role, find the person and choose the **Duration**.
3. Press **Add**.

Renewing a role:

1. Find the person on the **Roles** page.
2. Press **Renew** and pick the new end date. Roles end by themselves, so renew early.

Keeping the records:

1. Open the **Activity log** page.
2. Press **Export JSON** and put the file in RSG's Drive once a year. The panel keeps only the last year.

## Common problems

- If a warning bar shows at the top of the panel, look at the **Connections** page. An account may have disconnected.
- When usage reaches 70% and 90%, the admins get an e-mail.
- If saved content is not on the site, look at `docs/handover/troubleshooting.md`.
