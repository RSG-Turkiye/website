You write and send e-mail as RSG. You always press send yourself.

## What you can do

- On the **E-mail** page you write a message in the **New email** tab.
- In the **Sent** tab you see what you sent.
- In the **Inbox** tab you see only your own threads, and you answer with **Reply**.

## What you cannot do, and who to ask

- An admin manages the authorised senders and the attachments.
- One e-mail has at most 10 recipients. You can send 20 e-mails an hour and 100 a day.
- A scheduled e-mail goes out only between 08:00 and 22:00 (Istanbul time).
- Your role lasts at most one month, then is renewed. Ask an admin to renew it.
- Your AI assistant cannot send e-mail or messages.

## Common tasks

Sending an e-mail:

1. Open the **New email** tab.
2. Write the recipients, the subject and the text.
3. Press **Send**.

Answering a reply:

1. Open the **Inbox** tab and choose the thread.
2. Press **Reply**, write and send.

## Common problems

- If **Send** does not work, you may have reached the hourly or daily limit. Wait a little.
- If a Gmail warning shows at the top of the panel, the site's e-mail has stopped. Tell an admin, who fixes it.
- If it says **Send failed.**, copy your text and tell an admin.
