You manage the symposium site's content and the certificates.

## What you can do

The Symposium page has these tabs:

- Edition settings.
- Speakers.
- Sessions.
- Committee.
- Certificates: after the symposium you upload the certificate PDFs and the CSV file.

## What you cannot do, and who to ask

- To show an announcement on the site, ask the announcer or an admin.
- The role lasts at most 12 months. An admin renews it before it ends.
- Your AI assistant cannot reach this section.
- An admin solves technical problems with the site itself.

## Common tasks

Adding or changing a speaker:

1. Open the Speakers tab.
2. Add or edit the person and save.
3. Wait a few minutes for it to show on the site.

Uploading certificates:

1. Open the Certificates tab.
2. Upload the CSV file (with the columns email, name, type, file) and the PDF files only.
3. Participants reach their certificates on the symposium site with their e-mail address.

## Common problems

- If your change is not on the site, wait until the site is rebuilt. If it still is not there, tell an admin.
- Certificates are deleted 30 days after upload, with the e-mail addresses. You do not need to delete them.
- If a certificate is not found, check the e-mail address in the CSV.
