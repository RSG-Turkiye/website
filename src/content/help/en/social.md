You prepare social media posts. The publisher decides what goes out.

## What you can do

- On the Social media page you see the Calendar, Undated drafts, Published and Event mode tabs.
- You prepare posts and plan slots, and edit and cancel your own posts.
- You pass a draft to a publisher with Send for approval. Drafts save themselves.

## What you cannot do, and who to ask

- You cannot approve or schedule a post. A social media publisher does that.
- You only read other people's posts.
- If your role has ended, ask an admin to renew it. This role lasts at most one month, then is renewed.

## Common tasks

Preparing a new post:

1. Press a day in the Calendar, or Add content.
2. Write the text and add the image (JPEG or PNG). Alt text is optional but recommended.
3. When the checklist is complete, choose Send for approval.

Fixing a post that was sent back:

1. Open the post and read the publisher's note.
2. Fix it and choose Send for approval again.

## Common problems

- Send for approval does not work: look at what is missing in the checklist.
- If you edit a scheduled post, it goes back to approval.
- If someone else changed the post, autosave stops. It waits until you press Save.
- If a post does not appear, look in Published. If there is a connection problem, tell an admin.
