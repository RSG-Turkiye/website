You prepare social media posts. The publisher decides whether a post goes out.

## What you can do

- On the **Social media** page you see the **Calendar**, **Undated drafts**, **Published** and **Event mode** tabs.
- You prepare posts, and edit and cancel your own posts.
- You can add a plan slot to a day in the calendar. A plan slot means a post is expected that day.
- You pass a draft to a publisher with **Send for approval**. Drafts save themselves.

## What you cannot do, and who to ask

- You cannot approve or schedule a post. A social media publisher does that.
- You only read other people's posts.
- Your role lasts at most one month, then is renewed. If it has ended, ask an admin to renew it.

## Common tasks

Preparing a new post:

1. In the **Calendar** tab press a day, or press **Add content**.
2. Write the text and add the image (it must be JPEG or PNG). Alt text, which says what is in the image, is good to have but not required.
3. The **Before it can go out** list at the bottom shows what is missing. When everything is done, press **Send for approval**.

Fixing a post that was sent back:

1. Open the post and read the publisher's note.
2. Fix it and press **Send for approval** again.

## Common problems

- If **Send for approval** does not work, look at the **Before it can go out** list. It says what is missing.
- If you edit a scheduled post, it goes back to approval.
- If someone else changed the post, autosave stops. It waits until you press **Save**.
- If a post does not appear, look in the **Published** tab. If there is a connection problem, tell an admin.
