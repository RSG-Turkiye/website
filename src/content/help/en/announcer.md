You write the announcements shown on the site.

## What you can do

On the **Announcements** page you add, edit and delete announcements. Each one has:

- A title and a description.
- An optional button text and button URL.
- An expiry date.
- A site: **Main site** or **Symposium site**.
- The **Also show as popup** box.

## What you cannot do, and who to ask

- To reach any other section, ask an admin for the matching role.
- Your role lasts up to 12 months or is permanent. If it has an end date, an admin renews it before then.
- Your AI assistant cannot write announcements. Only you can.

## Common tasks

Adding an announcement:

1. On the **Announcements** page, write the title and the description.
2. Choose the site and the expiry date. Add a button if you want one.
3. Press **Save announcement**.

Changing or removing an announcement:

1. Find it in the list.
2. Press **Edit**, change it and save. To remove it, press **Delete**.

## Common problems

- If an announcement shows on the wrong site, check the **Site** field.
- If an announcement has disappeared, its expiry date may have passed.
- If you do not want a popup, clear the **Also show as popup** box.
