# Image credits

Not under `public/`, deliberately: everything there is served, so a
provenance note placed beside an image becomes a page. This is a repository
record.

## `public/images/metu-campus.jpg`

Photographed by **Samet**, a member of RSG-Türkiye. Supplied to the
organisers and cleared for use on this site by Emre Çevik on 2026-09-10.

The caption calls it the METU campus rather than the U3 amphitheatre. It
was offered as a picture of the hall and may well be one — three brick
volumes under copper roofs is what a triple amphitheatre should look like —
but nobody has yet confirmed which building it is. "Campus" is true either
way. Ask Samet: he took it, so he is the one person who can say.

`venue.photoCaption` in `src/i18n/ui.ts` is the string to change, both
languages, if the building is identified or the photograph is replaced.
